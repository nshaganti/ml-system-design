Group H - Position-bias debiasing (Phase 11, simulation)

Naive CTR ranks positions as much as items. IPW divides out the slot effect and recovers the true ranking - even with the examination curve estimated from a randomization bucket. (Controlled sim: Pure logs no position.)

Group I - Two-stage integration: retrieve + rank (Phase 12)

Honest architecture result: two-tower -> LR rerank LOSES to two-tower alone (popularity-flavored ranker undoes personalization) but beats popularity -> LR. A pipeline is only as good as the signal stage 2 adds.

Group J - Stage 2 earns its place: two-tower score as a feature (Phase 13)

The fix for Group I: feeding the retrieval score into the LR flips the -19% regression into a +3.3% win over two-tower alone. Two stages beat one only once stage 2 can see what stage 1 knows.

Group K - Explore-and-learn: where unbiased data comes from (Phase 14)

Mean cumulative regret over 50k rounds (60 seeded worlds). Thompson's curve FLATTENS (it converges and stops paying) while greedy/epsilon stay linear. Exploration is the online source of the unbiased data Part II needs.
